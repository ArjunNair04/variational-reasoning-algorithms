# Q5 prior-exponent screen: results

## Summary

Halving the rationale-prior exponent with a moving answer reader produced a
small improvement over its matched Q5 control. Removing that factor altogether
spread responsibility across more traces but reduced accuracy. Freezing the
answer reader did not improve either new setting.

The following means use the same three seeds, 1201, 1213 and 1217. The tau=1
controls and frozen baseline come from historical run `68078ecc`; the four new
settings come from run `e97c3a20`. These are development-validation results, not
official-test results or a seven-seed confirmation.

| Answer reader | Prior exponent | Final Acc@1 | Strict final | Acc@1 AUC |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Frozen, historical control | 1 | 81.67% | 71.08% | 79.89% |
| Moving | 0.5 | 80.75% | 69.83% | 80.09% |
| Moving, historical control | 1 | 80.17% | 69.25% | 79.30% |
| Frozen | 0.5 | 80.17% | 68.42% | 79.10% |
| Frozen | 0 | 77.67% | 66.17% | 78.62% |
| Moving | 0 | 77.58% | 64.75% | 78.65% |
| Frozen base, no training | Not applicable | 75.50% | 61.50% | 75.50% |

Final Acc@1 uses conventional extracted-answer correctness; strict final uses
the registered strict-answer event. AUC is normalized over rounds 0-32, using
the matched frozen baseline at round zero. The table is sorted by Final Acc@1.

## Controlled Change

For a rationale h, responsibilities are proportional to

`p_reader(answer + EOS | question, h) * p_current(h | question)^tau`.

Only tau and the moving-versus-frozen answer reader change. Proposal sampling
remains answer-derived; the buffer remains a persistent set of up to 16 unique
traces per question; and the joint weighted M-step is unchanged. Every cell
uses 128 optimization questions, the fixed 400-question validation pool,
three-shot prompts, rank-16 attention-plus-MLP LoRA, 32 rounds, 2,048 training
generations and 32 optimizer steps. Tau is a prior exponent, not a generation
temperature or an importance-sampling correction.

## Matched Changes

All changes below are percentage points relative to the same-reader tau=1
control on the same seeds, not relative to the seven-seed panel.

| Setting | Final change | Strict-final change | AUC change |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Moving, tau=0.5 | +0.58 | +0.58 | +0.79 |
| Moving, tau=0 | -2.58 | -4.50 | -0.66 |
| Frozen, tau=0.5 | -1.50 | -2.67 | -0.79 |
| Frozen, tau=0 | -4.00 | -4.92 | -1.27 |

Moving tau=0.5 changed Final Acc@1 by +1.25, -0.75 and +1.25 points across
the three seeds. The registered descriptive bootstrap interval is therefore
[-0.75, +1.25] points. The gain is small and is not consistent across all seeds.

Under the frozen nomination rule, moving tau=0.5 is the only eligible new
setting: its mean final change is positive and its mean strict-final change is
nonnegative. Nomination is not confirmation. No extra seeds are launched
automatically. Historical frozen-reader tau=1 still has the highest endpoint
mean in this panel.

## What Changed in the Buffer

These diagnostics average question-round records from all three seeds.

| Setting | Largest trace weight | Effective support (ESS) | Weight-length rank correlation | Top trace differs from tau=1 score on same support |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Moving, tau=0.5 | 89.11% | 1.28 | -0.397 | 4.44% |
| Frozen, tau=0.5 | 89.55% | 1.27 | -0.420 | 3.40% |
| Moving, tau=0 | 28.30% | 6.09 | +0.022 | 83.59% |
| Frozen, tau=0 | 28.63% | 6.18 | -0.014 | 82.25% |

Removing the prior largely removes the preference for shorter traces and
changes the highest-weight trace in most buffers. Nevertheless, both tau=0
settings perform worse. This argues against treating posterior concentration
or length bias alone as the cause of Q5's remaining errors. The experiment
does not separate the effects of flatter weights from changed trace ranking
and the later changes in sampled support.

Halving the prior leaves concentration high and rarely changes the top trace.
Its moving-reader gain is compatible with a modest reweighting benefit, not a
wholesale repair of buffer support. Freezing the answer reader adds no observed
benefit at tau=0.5 or tau=0 in this screen.
